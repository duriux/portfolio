A few words
about me.

I'm Julien Amiel, a Master's student in Operations Research at Columbia University, in New York City. Before Columbia, I studied Mathematics and Computer Science at Université Paris Dauphine-PSL.

My work sits at the intersection of quantitative modeling and human-centered design. I believe the most interesting problems aren't purely technical — they live in the friction between systems and the people who use them, especially when those systems break.

I'm drawn to projects that bring rigor to messy, real-world questions: how cities respond to disruption, how communities stay connected when infrastructure fails, how design choices ripple through decisions made by thousands of people at once.

Outside the classroom, I prototype, sketch, write, and learn from people whose problems I don't yet understand.
