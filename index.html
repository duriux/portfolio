02 — Approach

Designing for people, with rigor.

01

Listen first

Every project starts with interviews and lived observation. The math comes after the people.

02

Model the system

Coming from operations research, I treat human behavior as part of a wider, optimizable system — not noise.

03

Build to learn

Prototypes exist to be wrong. Each iteration sharpens the question more than it answers it.

04

Design for fairness

Solutions that only work for the well-served aren't solutions. Equity is a design constraint, not an afterthought.
